B.Tech Artificial Intelligence and Data Science Capstone Project

SVJ MART

Smart Shopping for Every Home • Enterprise E-Commerce Platform

Executive Summary

SVJ MART is a production-grade full-stack supermarket and home-essentials e-commerce platform built as a capstone project for the Bachelor of Technology in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science. The application unifies a comprehensive 16-aisle catalogue of everyday essentials tailored for Indian households with advanced machine-learning and heuristic recommendation engines, strict server-side transaction concurrency, multi-role access control, and real-time shipment status tracking.

AI & Data Science Methodologies

1. Content-Based Affinity Engine

Computes multidimensional similarity vectors across categories, nutritional attributes, dietary flags (Vegetarian vs Non-Veg), and brand loyalty to generate tailored storefront recommendations.

2. Market Basket Co-Occurrence Mining

Implements an association rule mining heuristic ("Frequently Bought Together") identifying culinary dependencies like Atta & Ghee, Dals & Sambar spices, Bread & Jams, and Cleaning solutions.

3. Dynamic Budget Knapsack Optimizer

Given a user's budgetary constraint (e.g., ₹500, ₹1000, ₹2000), the optimizer solves a constrained greedy knapsack problem to curate a nutrition-balanced grocery basket maximizing utility without exceeding the financial limit.

4. Conversational NLP Recipe Resolver

Extracts intents from natural language queries (e.g. "What do I need for Pav Bhaji?", "High-protein breakfast") to resolve exact inventory ingredients and render instant 1-click cart addition.
